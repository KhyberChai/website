Before you visit

A few useful things.

Which location should I choose?

Vancouver is our downtown chai house, serving chai, parathas, rolls and bowls. Surrey is our dine-in restaurant, with grilled dishes, curries and weekend brunch. Each location has its own menu and ordering page.

Can I order for pickup or delivery?

Use the Order Online button for our current ordering page, or order through the Vancouver Uber Eats listing. Availability and delivery charges are shown there.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes. The menus include paneer dishes, parathas and other vegetarian choices. The selection is different at each location, so check that location’s menu. Let our team know about any dietary requirements before ordering.

Can I order catering?

Yes. Send us the event date, number of guests, preferred location and what you have in mind. We’ll get back to you to discuss the menu and arrangements.
