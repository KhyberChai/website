A little Peshawar in BC

Chai ho jaye?

Shall we have some chai?

That’s the question on the wall at our Surrey restaurant. It’s also a good way to begin a visit to Khyber.

Peshawari food and traditional chai are at the heart of Khyber. Our menu brings together Peshawari breakfasts, Pakistani curries, grilled meats and Afghan favourites. The chai house in Vancouver keeps things a little simpler, with chai, parathas, rolls and bowls.

Come as you are. Tell us what you’re in the mood for and we’ll help you choose.

Before you visit

A few useful things.

Which location should I choose?

Vancouver is our downtown chai house, serving chai, parathas, rolls and bowls. Surrey is our dine-in restaurant, with grilled dishes, curries and weekend brunch. Each location has its own menu and ordering page.

When is brunch served?

Weekend brunch is served at our Surrey location from 11 AM to 3 PM on Saturdays and Sundays. The brunch menu also lists holiday service. Check the booking form for available tables.

Do I need a reservation?

You can request a table at Surrey through our reservation form. Your booking is complete only after the restaurant confirms it. For questions, call 604-593-0080.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes. The menus include paneer dishes, parathas and other vegetarian choices. The selection is different at each location, so check that location’s menu. Let our team know about any dietary requirements before ordering.

Can I order catering?

Yes. Send us the event date, number of guests, preferred location and what you have in mind. We’ll get back to you to discuss the menu and arrangements.
